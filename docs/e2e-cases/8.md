# End-to-end cases for #8 — Pure effective-price and promotion precedence functions

Covers: R3, R4. Derived from the issue's acceptance criteria, not from the
implementation. Case ids are permanent.

Note on criterion 3: the issue body says the product-level candidate is applied
whenever both exist. The owner has since settled promotion precedence as the
lower effective price winning, in the customer's favour (ADR-0004, owner
decision 2026-09-12). Case 8-3 is written against the settled policy, and 8-3b
records the product-level override as the admin's deliberate escape hatch.

Note on 8-3 and 8-3b: the story names the resolver `resolveApplied` in the
effective-price module. Precedence is no longer resolved in code — ADR-0004
gives it to the `json-rules-engine` rules in `pricing_rules`, and issue #36 owns
that resolver — so neither case is runnable on this branch and both skip. They
are written against the story's own name deliberately; nothing here should
re-add a `resolveApplied` function in order to satisfy them.

### 8-1

- Actor: shopper opening a product that carries a percentage promotion, who needs the price they see to be the price that applies
- Covers: criterion 1 of #8
- Precondition: `applyPromotion` in `src/modules/promotion/effective-price.ts`
- Given: a base price of 10 000 cents and an active promotion of 25 % (2 500 basis points)
- When: the promotion is applied to the base price
- Then: the effective price is 7 500 cents
- And Given: a base price of 1 000 cents and an active promotion of 33.33 % (3 333 basis points)
- And Then: the effective price is 667 cents — the discount is floored, not rounded
- Measure: none

### 8-2

- Actor: shopper opening a product whose fixed discount exceeds its price
- Covers: criterion 2 of #8
- Precondition: `applyPromotion` in `src/modules/promotion/effective-price.ts`
- Given: a base price of 500 cents and an active fixed promotion of 800 cents
- When: the promotion is applied to the base price
- Then: the effective price is 0 cents, never negative
- Measure: none

### 8-3

- Actor: shopper listing a promoted category whose product also carries its own promotion, who must be charged the better of the two
- Covers: criterion 3 of #8
- Precondition: the rule-engine resolver the story calls `resolveApplied`; not in this tree (issue #36) — this case skips
- Given: one active product-level candidate and one active category-level candidate for the same product
- When: the applied promotion is resolved
- Then: the candidate producing the lower effective price is applied, and its effective price is returned
- And Given: only a category-level candidate is active
- And Then: that candidate is applied
- And Given: no candidate is active
- And Then: the applied promotion is `null` and the price is the base price
- Measure: none

### 8-3b

- Actor: admin who has set a deliberate product price and needs it to survive a category flash sale
- Covers: criterion 3 of #8 (owner's settled precedence policy, ADR-0004)
- Precondition: the rule-engine resolver the story calls `resolveApplied`, plus a higher-priority `pricing_rules` row naming the product level; not in this tree (issue #36) — this case skips
- Given: an active product-level candidate priced above an active category-level candidate, and a higher-priority rule naming the product level
- When: the applied promotion is resolved
- Then: the product-level candidate is applied, overriding the lower-price default
- Measure: none

### 8-4

- Actor: admin starting a flash sale, who needs it live when they said and not a moment before
- Covers: criterion 4 of #8
- Precondition: `isActive` in `src/modules/promotion/effective-price.ts`
- Given: a promotion with `status === 'active'` and a `now`
- When: `isActive(promotion, now)` is evaluated
- Then: it is true exactly when `startsAt <= now < endsAt`; `now === startsAt` is active and `now === endsAt` is not
- And Given: the same window with `status` of `draft`, then with `status` of `cancelled`
- And Then: `isActive` is false in both cases — a draft or a cancelled promotion is never active
- Measure: none
